Nine Lives – Privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026

Nine Lives is a skateboarding game made by PJ Apps. It works for the Android app "Nine Lives" (com.pjapps.ninelives) and the browser version at peter23023.github.io/ninelives. This page explains what information the game uses and why. In short: there are no accounts, and we don't sell your data.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: best scores, fish coins, outfits, hats, upgrades, missions and settings are saved only on your device. If you have Android backup switched on, Android may include them in your own Google backup so they come back on a new phone.
  • Replay videos and mugshot pictures are made on your device. A video you save goes to your phone's Movies folder. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share and choose an app yourself.

Ads (Android app)

The Android app can show rewarded ads from Google AdMob, only when you tap "Keep going" or "Double fish" after a run. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested; you can change it any time in Settings > Privacy options. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings),
  • approximate location from your IP address,
  • information about your device and the app, and how you interact with the ads (including diagnostics).

How Google uses this: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Leaderboards

  • Android app (Google Play Games): if you sign in to Google Play Games, your Play Games player ID and your best score are sent to Google to show the friends leaderboard. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Browser version (team scoreboard): if you join a team scoreboard with its code, the name you type and your best score each month are stored with Google Firebase, together with a random anonymous ID made for your browser (no e-mail, no account). Only people with the team code see the board.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, no e-mail address, no contacts, no precise location, no microphone or camera.
  • No in-app purchases. Links to the Nine Lives clothing shop (when it opens) only go to a website for real clothes; buying there never gives anything in the game.
  • We don't sell personal data.

Children

Nine Lives is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app (or clearing the browser's data for the site) removes everything stored on your device. To have a scoreboard entry removed, contact us through the developer e-mail shown on the game's Google Play page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
